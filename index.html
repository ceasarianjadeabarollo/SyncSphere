SyncSphere Features

Everything you need to ace your semester

See your semester at a glance.

SyncSphere brings your entire academic workflow into one seamless space. Organize your daily priorities with an intuitive to-do list, align deadlines across your schedule and calendar, and keep subject resources structured under dedicated course hubs. When it is time to study, jump straight into distraction-free work using the built-in Pomodoro timer—turning your routine into a balanced, focused rhythm.

  • To-Do: Instant daily overview of your active tasks and urgent priorities.
  • Schedule: Clear weekly planning for your regular classes and recurring routines.
  • Calendar: Unified timeline for upcoming deadlines, exam dates, and major commitments.
  • Courses: Smarter organization with all your subject materials stored in one place—less time spent searching.
  • Pomodoro: Smarter focus through distraction-free study intervals tied directly to your work.

And so much more!

How it works

A simple three-step rhythm to organize and ace your semester

Step 01

1. Plan & Schedule

Schedule & Calendar

Map out your day-to-day timeline. Use the schedule to manage recurring weekly routines (such as lectures or work blocks) while the calendar handles specific dates, assignment deadlines, and milestone events.

Todo Lists

Break broad goals into actionable items. Categorize by priority, set due dates, and check off tasks as you complete them to keep your momentum going.

Step 02

2. Learn & Retain

Courses Hub

Organize your academic life by subject. Store syllabi, track specific course modules, and view deadlines associated with each class in one place.

Notes

Capture ideas, lecture notes, or project outlines right beside your course materials, eliminating the friction of switching between apps.

Resource Links

Pin essential bookmarks, class portals, research papers, and shared drives directly inside the app for one-click access.

Step 03

3. Focus & Execute

Pomodoro Timer

Turn your to-do lists into focused execution sessions. Work in customizable 25-minute intervals paired with short breaks to prevent burnout and maintain steady flow.

How the Flow Connects

Set Up

Add your enrolled Courses and bookmark relevant Links.

Organize

Enter deadlines into your Calendar, set up routine blocks in your Schedule, and jot down detailed study materials in Notes.

Prioritize

Extract action items into your daily Todo list.

Execute

Launch the Pomodoro timer to power through tasks uninterrupted.
